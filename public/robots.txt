# Application routes (/exercise, /exercise-stats, /statistics, /auth, /profile,
# /verify-magic-link, /admin, /email-preferences) are deliberately NOT disallowed:
# they serve <meta name="robots" content="noindex">, and crawlers must be able to
# fetch a URL to see its noindex. A robots.txt block would leave them eligible
# to appear as URL-only search results (INDEX-015). Their parameterised forms
# (e.g. /exercise?level=B1) remain covered by the parameter policy below.

# Major Search Engines
User-agent: Googlebot
# Parameter crawl policy: calendar, session, and tracking parameter URL
# spaces are non-canonical. The indexable surface (public/sitemap.xml) is
# path-only; every indexable page emits a parameter-free canonical.
