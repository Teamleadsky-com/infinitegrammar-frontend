How I work

Three habits that run through every article.

Evidence before opinions

Every change to what users see gets a pass rule written before the test, a decision ID and its evidence linked. Ideas I liked lost, and that’s in the log too.

Quality is a product decision

I define “good” as checks and scores first, test the judge on known answers, and let cost per clean output pick the model.

Agents build, I own the risk

Claude Code writes most of the code. Decisions, budgets, production data and the final read of the output stay with me.
